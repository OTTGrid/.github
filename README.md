**OTTGrid Engine** is the streaming engine for OTT and IPTV operators. You install it on your own server, add your
licensed channels and movies, and it delivers them to your subscribers' apps, Smart TVs and set-top boxes. It is written
in Rust, with FFmpeg 8.0 built for it.

- **Live and on demand**: live streams with low-latency HLS, 24/7 channels from your own videos, movies and series
  filled in from TMDb, radio stations and recordings.
- **Subscribers and resellers**: lines, MAG and Enigma2 devices, packages, and a Reseller Portal where your resellers
  sell with credits.
- **Scale out**: Edge Nodes installed from the Admin Console, Edge Cluster to balance your channels across them, and
  Proxy Nodes in front.
- **Move in with one command**: `tools migrate` brings your channels, lines and resellers over from XUI.one, Xtream UI,
  1-Stream or NXT, and your subscribers keep their logins.
